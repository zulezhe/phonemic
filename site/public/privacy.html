1. The short version

PhoneMic turns your phone into a microphone for your computer. We do not run an account system, we do not have analytics, and we never receive your audio. There is nothing to sell and nothing to profile.

Your voice never reaches us in a readable form. Audio is encrypted on your phone and decrypted on your own computer.

2. What happens to your audio

When you scan the pairing code, the desktop app and the phone page derive a shared session key from the pairing code together with an ECDH key exchange, using HKDF-SHA256.

Everything you speak is encrypted on the phone with AES-256-GCM before it leaves the device, and decrypted only on your computer. The relay server forwards encrypted packets between the two devices. It cannot decrypt them, and it does not write audio to disk or to logs.

On the same local network, PhoneMic prefers a direct connection between your phone and your computer, so the audio does not pass through any server at all.

No recording is created at any point. The stream is live: audio that has been played on your computer is discarded and cannot be retrieved later, not even by you.

3. What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Specifically:

  • No account data — there is no sign-up, no email, no password, no license key.
  • No analytics or telemetry — the app does not phone home, and neither the app nor this website includes an analytics SDK, advertising script, tracking pixel or third-party font.
  • No device identifiers — we do not collect advertising IDs, IMEI, MAC addresses or hardware fingerprints.
  • No audio, no transcripts — see section 2.
  • No crash reports — the app contains no automatic crash-reporting service.

If you email us for support, we receive whatever you choose to include in that message (typically your email address, macOS or Windows version, and a description of the problem). We use it only to answer you and delete it once the issue is closed, at the latest after 12 months.

4. Data stored on your device

The desktop app stores a small amount of configuration locally, on your own machine, and never uploads it:

  • Your interface language preference.
  • The display names of audio devices you have selected.
  • Whether the app should launch at login (this is a system setting).

Removing the app and its configuration folder deletes all of it. There is no cloud copy to delete.

5. Third-party components

BlackHole (macOS). PhoneMic uses the open-source BlackHole virtual audio driver (GNU GPL v3.0) to expose a microphone device to other applications. BlackHole runs entirely on your computer and does not transmit data anywhere.

VB-CABLE (Windows). The Windows build works with VB-CABLE, a third-party virtual audio driver by VB-Audio. It is not bundled with PhoneMic and is not operated by us; you install it from the official VB-Audio website, and its own terms and privacy practices apply.

Relay infrastructure. The public relay runs on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects. Like any network provider, Cloudflare processes connection metadata (IP address, timestamps) necessary to deliver traffic, under its own privacy policy. Because traffic is end-to-end encrypted, the relay operator — including us — cannot see what is being transmitted. If you self-host the relay, no third party is involved at all.

6. This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on Cloudflare. It sets no advertising or tracking cookies, loads no external scripts, and embeds no fonts from third-party servers.

Your browser stores one value in local storage — your language choice — so the page opens in the same language next time. It stays on your device and is never transmitted.

Cloudflare, as the hosting provider, automatically processes standard technical request data (such as IP address, user agent and the requested URL) to serve the pages and protect against abuse. We do not build profiles from it and do not combine it with any other data.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side. If you have sent us a support email and want it removed, write to the address below and we will delete it.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, California or another region with data-protection legislation, you retain all statutory rights over any data you have voluntarily sent us. We rely on your consent (when you email us) as the legal basis, and we do not sell or share personal information.

PhoneMic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
